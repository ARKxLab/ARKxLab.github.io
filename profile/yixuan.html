Yixuan Li

Yixuan Li is an undergraduate student at Beijing Forestry University and will pursue an M.Eng. in Electronic Information at Tsinghua University in 2025. She has collaborated with Tsinghua University’s Future Lab and School of Design, as well as the Southern University of Science and Technology, focusing on Human-Computer Interaction research.

Her current work explores haptic interaction and tangible interfaces that bring accessible, emotionally rich experiences to broader audiences.

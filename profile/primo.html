Dongyijie Primo Pan

Primo is an MPhil student at HKUST(GZ), under the supervision of Pan Hui Lab and Xin Tong Lab. He graduated from the Digital Media Art program at the Communication University of China and has previously worked as a software developer at companies like Tencent and NetEase. His research interests primarily lie in mixed reality and human-computer interaction applications in healthcare. He is particularly keen on using MR to aid in psychological therapy and daily health management. Additionally, he has a strong passion for exploring music creation in the AI era.

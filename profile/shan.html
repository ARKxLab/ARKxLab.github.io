Shan Luo

Shan Luo is a Research Assistant at ARK Lab. Her past research and publications have primarily focused on designing playful interactions and games to bridge the gap between the blind and the sighted.

She is now exploring how play can help build connections and better communication between humans and non-humans in nature.

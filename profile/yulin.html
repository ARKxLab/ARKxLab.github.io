Yulin Chen

Yulin Chen is a Research Assistant at ARK Lab, HKUST (Guangzhou).

Yulin Chen's research interests lie at the intersection of Human-Robot Interaction (HRI), Human-AI Interaction, and Embodied AI. The research focuses on how intelligent agents can perceive, understand, and reason about dynamic social contexts to make contextually appropriate decisions. By combining human-centered research methods with machine learning and robotics, Yulin aims to develop socially aware and adaptive AI systems that can interact effectively with people in complex, real-world environments.

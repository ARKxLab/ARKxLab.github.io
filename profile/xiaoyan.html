Xiaoyan Hu

MPhil Student

Xiaoyan Hu is currently pursuing her MPhil degree at HKUST(GZ). Her research lies in HCI and human-robot interaction, with a focus on pioneering interdisciplinary innovations that integrate XR technology with interactive experiences.

Research Interests

HCI, Human-Robot Interaction & Extended Reality

Pioneering interdisciplinary innovations that integrate XR technology with interactive experiences.

Human-Computer Interaction (HCI)

Exploring human-centered interaction paradigms and interdisciplinary innovations in interactive computing.

Human-Robot Interaction (HRI)

Investigating how humans and robotic systems interact and collaborate across interactive contexts.

Extended Reality (XR)

Integrating XR technology into novel interdisciplinary systems to bridge physical and virtual worlds.

Interactive Experiences

Designing engaging interactive experiences powered by XR and human-robot interaction technologies.

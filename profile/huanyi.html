Huanyi Wan

Huanyi Wan is an MPhil student (2026 Fall cohort) at ARK Lab, HKUST (Guangzhou). He holds a Bachelor of Arts degree in Art and Technology (Digital Entertainment) from Communication University of China, bringing an interdisciplinary background spanning game design, HCI research, and AI development.

His research focuses on human-AI collaboration, AR/VR systems, and assistive technologies for health and wellbeing. He has contributed to multiple cutting-edge HCI projects, spanning from designing interactive systems that integrate AI agents into AR and web applications to conducting qualitative user studies and developing novel human-AI interaction paradigms.

  • Email: huanyiwan@hkust-gz.edu.cn
Research Interests

Human-AI Collaboration and Assistive Technologies

Huanyi explores how AI and immersive technologies can empower users and improve quality of life.

Human-AI Collaboration

Designing interactive systems that leverage AI agents to support users' meaning-making processes, time management, and personalized interface generation.

AR/VR Systems

Developing augmented reality applications for social augmentation and assistive technologies, integrating AI agents into immersive environments.

Assistive Technologies

Creating technology-mediated interventions for neurodiverse individuals, focusing on workplace social communication support and accessibility.

Yancheng Cao

Yancheng Cao is a research assistant at ARK Lab, HKUST (Guangzhou). He holds a bachelor's degree from Wenzhou Medical University and a master's degree from Tongji University, bringing an interdisciplinary background to his research.

His research interests focus on AI-powered interactive systems for health support and design-thinking-based human-computer interaction interface design. His work has been published at top-tier HCI conferences including IUI and IMWUT.

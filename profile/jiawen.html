Jiawen Zhang

Jiawen Zhang is a PhD student at ARK Lab, HKUST (Guangzhou). She holds an MA in Service Design from the Royal College of Art and previously interned at Tsinghua University's Future Lab.

Her prior research applied generative AI to support autism education, and she has participated in multiple digital media art exhibitions, including interdisciplinary projects integrating AI with traditional embroidery and autism art. Her current research focuses on how digital technologies can empower the preservation, inheritance, and innovative dissemination of intangible cultural heritage.

Research Interests

Digital Heritage and Creative Technologies

Jiawen explores how digital technologies can preserve and innovate traditional cultural practices.

Intangible Cultural Heritage

Designing digital systems for the preservation, inheritance, and innovative dissemination of traditional cultural practices and knowledge.

Generative AI for Education

Applying generative AI technologies to support autism education and create inclusive learning experiences.

Interdisciplinary Art

Creating digital media art exhibitions that integrate AI with traditional crafts, such as embroidery, and autism art.

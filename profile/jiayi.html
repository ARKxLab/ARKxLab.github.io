Jiayi Huang (黄嘉怡)

Jiayi Huang is an MPhil student at the Thrust of Computational Media and Arts, HKUST(GZ). With a background in Intelligent Transportation from North China University of Technology, her current research focuses on pedagogy- and psychology-based interactive toys for STEM education in the AI era. She welcomes collaboration with researchers in related fields to define the next generation of smart toys.
